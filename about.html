Technology for a Wilder India

Exploring how modern technology helps protect India's forests, wildlife, and natural ecosystems.

Why It Matters

Protecting forests and wildlife requires understanding what happens across vast, changing ecosystems. Modern technology gives conservationists new ways to observe, investigate, and respond.

  1. 01. Observe

    Satellites, camera traps, and sensors help reveal changes in forests and wildlife activity.

  2. 02. Understand

    GPS, mapping systems, and AI help researchers interpret observations and identify patterns.

  3. 03. Protect

    These insights can support better-informed monitoring, planning, and conservation decisions.

The Project

TechForWild is an educational project exploring the role of modern tools in wildlife and forest conservation in India.

  • Research-based learning

    Learn about real technologies and their applications.

  • Conservation awareness

    Highlight the importance of protecting natural ecosystems.

  • Visual storytelling

    Bring the topic together through our documentary.
